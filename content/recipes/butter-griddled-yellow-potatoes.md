Boiled until fork-tender, steam-dried, then browned in butter on a hot griddle
for creamy interiors and golden-brown edges.

- **Yield:** Approximately 2–3 side servings
- **Total time:** Approximately 30–40 minutes, plus grill preheating

## Equipment

- Weber Searwood pellet grill with griddle attachment
- Medium saucepan
- Colander
- Griddle spatula
- Fork

## Ingredients

- 1 lb full-sized yellow potatoes
- 1–2 tbsp butter
- ½ tsp table salt per 4 cups boiling water
- Additional pinch of salt, to taste
- Water, enough to cover potatoes by 1 inch

## Instructions

1. **Preheat the grill:** Set the Weber Searwood to 400°F with the griddle
   attachment installed.
2. **Cut the potatoes:** Cut the yellow potatoes into approximately 1-inch
   chunks. Leave the skins on.
3. **Boil:** Place the potatoes in a saucepan and add enough cold water to cover
   them by approximately 1 inch. Add ½ teaspoon table salt per 4 cups of water.
4. **Simmer until tender:** Bring the water to a boil, then reduce to a gentle
   simmer. Cook for approximately 12–18 minutes, until a fork slides through the
   potatoes easily without resistance.
5. **Drain and steam-dry:** Drain the potatoes in a colander and let them
   steam-dry for approximately 2 minutes. This removes surface moisture and
   helps them brown on the griddle.
6. **Butter the griddle:** Add 1–2 tablespoons of butter to the hot griddle.
7. **Griddle the potatoes:** Transfer the drained potatoes to the griddle in a
   single layer. Cook for approximately 10–15 minutes, turning every 3–4 minutes
   to develop golden-brown surfaces.
8. **Season and serve:** Add a small pinch of salt to taste. Remove when the
   potatoes have golden-brown edges and creamy, fork-tender interiors.

## Doneness

The potatoes are fully cooked before they reach the griddle. The griddling stage
is for browning and flavor development.

- **Exterior:** Golden brown on at least one or two sides.
- **Interior:** Soft and creamy, with no firm center.
- **Fork test:** A fork slides effortlessly through the center.

## Notes

- Boiling before griddling produces a creamy interior without requiring a long
  griddle cook.
- Letting the potatoes steam-dry after boiling helps the surfaces brown.
- Turning every 3–4 minutes allows individual sides to develop color.
- The recipe uses very mild seasoning and works well for young children.
