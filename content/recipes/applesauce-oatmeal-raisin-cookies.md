Soft oatmeal raisin cookies made with cinnamon applesauce, rolled oats, and coconut oil.

- **Yield:** About 12-16 small cookies
- **Prep time:** 10 minutes
- **Bake time:** 10–12 minutes

## Ingredients
- 1 cup rolled oats
- 3/4 cup flour (whole wheat or all-purpose)
- 1/2 cup unsweetened cinnamon applesauce
- 1/4 cup melted coconut oil
- 1/4 cup raisins
- 1 egg
- 1-2 tbsp maple syrup or honey (*optional, depending how sweet you want*)
- 1/2 tsp baking soda
- 1/2 tsp cinnamon (*extra if you love it*)
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp vanilla extract

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 350°F. Line a baking sheet.
2. Mix wet ingredients: applesauce, coconut oil, egg, vanilla, and sweetener.
3. In another bowl, mix oats, flour, baking soda, cinnamon, and salt.
4. Combine everything and fold in raisins.
5. Scoop small spoonfuls and gently flatten (they don't spread much).
6. Bake 10–12 minutes until set but still soft.
