Tart cherry gummies made with beef gelatin and lightly sweetened with honey or maple syrup. Pour them into gummy molds or cut them into squares or shapes.

- **Chill time:** 30 minutes

## Ingredients
- 1 1/2 cups tart cherry juice
- 4 tbsp organic grass-fed beef gelatin powder
- 2-3 tbsp honey or maple syrup

## Instructions
1. Pour juice and honey into a small saucepan over medium-low heat. Stir until honey starts to dissolve.
2. Sprinkle gelatin over the juice mixture, whisking and heating as you go until most of the clumps have dissolved.
3. Pour mixture into a square 8×8 pan or pie plate (if doing squares or cookie cut-outs) or use a dropper if adding into silicone gummy molds.
4. Place in fridge for 30 minutes until mixture has solidified. Cut into shapes or squares. Keep stored in the fridge for up to a week.

## Notes
- Use silicone gummy molds for individual gummies, or pour the mixture into a pan and cut it into squares or shapes once set.
